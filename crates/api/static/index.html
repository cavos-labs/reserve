Three moves, one transaction.

  1. The sponsor carries the reserves

    A sandwich opens around the user’s operations. Their minimum balance stays at zero.

  2. The user signs the inner transaction

    Reserve never holds a key. It signs only the outer fee-bump, which cannot change what they signed.

  3. A path payment buys the XLM

    Strict-receive through the DEX. Past the quoted sendMax, nothing happens and they pay nothing.
